Duolingo: "Same Café"

Brief: Audience: adults aged 25 to 40 who plan to travel or live abroad and freeze when it's actually time to speak. · Feeling: relatable nerves turning into quiet pride, the warm "I can do this too" moment. · Idea: small daily lessons (on the train, in bed, at the stove) turn a frozen, menu-pointing tourist into someone who chats with the waiter at the same café. The app is the quiet hero behind her confidence.

Poppi: "The 3 PM Issue"

Brief: Audience: 20- to 35-year-olds at a desk all day who want a fun soda as an afternoon pick-me-up, without reaching for the usual. · Feeling: the treat-yourself moment, the small daily reward that turns a grey afternoon around. · Idea: shot like a magazine issue. The 3 PM slump is literally black and white, and one cold can brings back color, fizz and a smile. It closes on "Get yours."
