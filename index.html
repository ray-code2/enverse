FAQ

Questions, answered.

What if I don’t like the ad?

You don’t pay. Payment comes only after you approve your first ad. No contract.

How does payment work?

Once you approve your first ad, we send a PayPal invoice. Pay by card or PayPal, no PayPal account needed. Previews are watermarked, and final files are delivered once payment is in. No deposit, no contract.

How do revisions work?

Send all your notes in one round and we update every ad. Starter includes 2 rounds, Growth 3, and Brand 5 per month.

What’s in each package?

Starter, $149: 5 static ads in feed and story sizes, a bonus short video, 2 revisions. Bonus: every size, captions and pinned comments. 3 days.

Growth, $449: 10 static ads, 3 short videos, captions, 3 revisions. Bonus: 3 hook variations and an A/B testing guide. 5 days.

Brand, $995/mo: 20 static ads and 8 videos a month, A/B variants, 5 revisions per month, priority delivery. Bonus: 6 to 10 second motion versions and a sound brief.

Timing starts once we have your brief.

How long does delivery take?

Starter is delivered in 3 days and Growth in 5 days. Brand is an ongoing monthly collection, with priority delivery. Timing starts once we have your brief.

Can you promise sales?

No one honestly can. Results depend on your offer, price and targeting too. We promise ads crafted to be watched, and you only pay for ones you would run.

Who owns the ads?

You do, once paid. Run them anywhere, as often as you like.
