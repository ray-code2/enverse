04 Process

From call to ready-to-post.

Six clear steps. Quality comes before speed, and every ad is reviewed in full before it reaches you.

  1. 01 · Brief

    Brief

    A 15-minute call and short brief on your product, buyer and goal, confirmed before we create anything.

  2. 02 · Story

    Story

    We find the story in your brand and script every ad around one clear idea, scene by scene.

  3. 03 · Production

    Production

    We make your ads in feed and story sizes, with sound, captions and motion tuned per platform.

  4. 04 · Quality

    Quality

    Story, sound, captions and detail are checked first. If it is not ready, it does not ship.

  5. 05 · Approval

    Approval

    You review a watermarked preview of your first ad, then approve it or send notes for a revision round.

  6. 06 · Delivery

    Delivery

    We send a secure PayPal invoice. Once paid, you receive every final file plus your package bonus.
